7By.in was built on a simple idea: powerful audio processing should be free, private, and work without uploading your files anywhere.

Why we built 7By.in

Existing vocal remover tools required you to upload your music to their servers, wait in queues, and often pay before even trying the result. That felt wrong.

With modern browser APIs, we realized the entire audio processing pipeline — separation, cutting, joining, pitch shifting — could all live in one place. No uploads to wait on, no queue to sit in.

We built 7By.in to prove that privacy-first audio tools could also be the best audio tools.

What makes it different

Most audio tools send your music to a server and make you wait in a queue. We took the harder route so you don't have to.

01
True separation, not stereo tricks

Our engine was trained to recognise what a voice, a drum kit and a bass line actually sound like — so it lifts them apart cleanly, even on mono recordings where older tricks fail outright.

02
Your music is never shared with anyone

Files are opened, processed and exported entirely on your own machine. We never receive your audio, so there is nothing for us to store, scan or lose.

03
No queues, Private wait

Because there is Instant start doing the work, there is no line to stand in. Processing starts the moment you press the button and uses whatever power your device has.
