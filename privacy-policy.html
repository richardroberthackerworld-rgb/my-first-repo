1. Overview

7By.in ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at https://7by.in. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

The most important thing to know: All audio processing on 7By.in runs entirely in your browser. Your audio files are never uploaded to our servers. We never see, store, or process your audio content.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you contact us via email (contact@7by.in), we receive your name, email address, and message content. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we may collect standard web log information including your IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. This is used for security and analytics purposes.

2.3 Cookies and Local Storage

We store your free credit usage for the day (for example, "10 of 20 credits used") so the free tier can reset correctly. For signed-in accounts this balance is held against your account; for guests it is kept on your own device and is never transmitted to us. We also use cookies for Google AdSense (see Section 5) and to remember your preferences.

2.4 Audio Files

Audio files you upload to 7By.in are processed entirely within your browser on your own device. These files are never sent to our servers, never stored by us, and are discarded when you close the tab or reset the tool.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal requirement: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to 7By.in and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please email us at contact@7by.in. Since we collect very limited personal data, in most cases this will simply mean deleting your email correspondence with us.
