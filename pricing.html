Frequently Asked Questions

Which tools are free?
Audio Cutter, Song Joiner, Pitch Shifter and Audio Converter are completely free and unlimited — no account, no credits. The three AI tools — Vocal Remover, Stem Splitter and Noise Remover — use credits, because AI processing is far heavier to run.
How do credits work?
AI separation costs 10 credits per song — that is 10 credits per 5 minutes of audio. You are only charged when you download, so previewing is always free, and re-downloading something you already paid for costs nothing. Everyone gets 20 free credits every day.
Do I need an account?
Not to try the free tools. Sign in when you want credits tied to your account rather than to one device, and so a paid top-up follows you across devices.
Do unused credits roll over?
Monthly credits reset each month. Annual credits stay valid for the full year. The 20 free daily credits do not carry over — they refresh every day.
What payment methods work?
UPI, cards, net banking and wallets are all supported at checkout. Credits appear on your account as soon as the payment goes through. Credits are non-refundable once added.
