Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience. We also use browser localStorage, which works similarly but persists differently than cookies.

2. How We Use Cookies and localStorage

2.1 Essential localStorage (First-Party)

We use localStorage in your browser to store:

  • 7by_free — stores the date and count of your daily free song usage (e.g., {"date":"2026-07-01","count":1}). This never leaves your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

This is essential to provide the free tier functionality. Without it, we cannot enforce the "20 free credits per day" limit fairly. You can clear this by clearing your browser's site data for 7by.in.

2.2 Google AdSense Cookies (Third-Party)

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history across the web. These cookies are set by Google, not by us. They may include:

For more information about Google's use of cookies in advertising, visit Google's advertising policies.

2.3 Analytics (Optional)

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. If enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga (2 years) and _gid (24 hours) to distinguish users and sessions. This data is anonymised and aggregated.

3. Controlling Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies

Note: Clearing localStorage for 7by.in will reset your daily free usage counter.

4. Opt-Out of Personalised Ads

To opt out of personalised advertising by Google, visit:

  • Google Ad Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out
  • Your Online Choices (EU)
