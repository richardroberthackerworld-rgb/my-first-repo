Frequently asked questions

Which video tools are free?
The ClipCut video editor, the video compressor, and the trim/convert/GIF toolkit are completely free and unlimited — no account, no credits, and no watermark on anything you export. Only the two AI tools, the Watermark Remover and the Background Remover, use credits, because AI processing is heavier to run.
How do credits work?
The AI Watermark Remover and Background Remover cost 2 credits per minute of video — so a clip up to 1 minute is 2 credits, and a 10-minute video is 20 credits. You're only charged when the tool finishes a successful run; previewing is always free. Everyone gets 20 free credits every day.
Do I need an account?
Not to use the free tools or your 20 daily free credits. Sign in when you want credits tied to your account rather than to one device, so a paid top-up follows you everywhere. Sign-in is with Google or a real Gmail address.
Do unused credits roll over?
Monthly credits reset each month. Annual credits stay valid for the full year. The 20 free daily credits don't carry over — they refresh every day.
Is my video uploaded anywhere?
No. Every VidLab tool processes your video on your own device, in your browser — your footage never leaves your computer. Credits simply meter access to the AI tools.
What payment methods work?
UPI, cards, net banking and wallets are all supported at checkout via Razorpay. Credits appear on your account as soon as the payment goes through. Credits are non-refundable once added.
