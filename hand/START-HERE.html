Try it with your handwriting

Twenty minutes. No printing, no scanner — a phone photo of something you already wrote is enough.

1 · Learn my handwriting →Upload a photo of a page you wrote 2 · Write with it →Type anything, download a PDF

Photograph a page you already wrote

Any page of your handwriting. Old notes, a past assignment, anything.

  • Print-style beats joined-up cursive. Letters that touch get discarded — the software only uses words where it can tell the letters apart. Cursive still works, it just keeps fewer words per page.
  • Dark pen, light paper. Blue or black. Not pencil, not red.
  • Flat and evenly lit. Daylight near a window. Avoid a hard shadow across the page.
  • Fill the frame, straight on. A slight tilt is corrected automatically.
  • Include capitals, numbers and punctuation somewhere. This matters more than you'd think — see the warning below.

Learn your handwriting

  1. Open learn.html and choose your photo.
  2. Press Analyse page. Boxes appear over the writing: lines, words, letters, baseline. If those look wrong, everything after will be wrong — adjust Speck floor or Word gap and analyse again.
  3. Type what the page says, one line in the box per line on the page. This is the only manual step.
  4. Press Build style, then Download style. You get a .json file.

A third to half of your words will be skipped, and that is correct. It only uses words it is certain about. One wrongly labelled letter would mean every future “a” is secretly an “o”, and nothing would ever catch it.

Write with it

  1. Open app.html → Load style file → pick your .json.
  2. Type or paste anything.
  3. Set Writing size and Line spacing to match your notebook. 8.5 mm is standard Indian ruled paper.
  4. Download PDF and print at 100% scale — not “fit to page”.

Reroll gives a different variation of the same text. The seed is saved, so the same seed always reproduces exactly the same page.

The thing that will probably go wrong

Whatever your photo doesn’t contain, your style won’t have. Photograph a page of all lowercase and you get lowercase-only handwriting. Type a capital T and it is skipped — the app lists which characters are missing.

Fix: write one extra line with the missing pieces, photograph it, and run it through learn.html as a second page:

ABCDEFGHIJKLM NOPQRSTUVWXYZ
0123456789 . , ; : ! ? ' " ( ) -

Space the letters out so they don’t touch — more of them survive.

What to change

To just test it: nothing. It works as-is. You type the transcript yourself.

To have it read the page for you, pick one:

Real hostingapi/config.phpPHP hosting only Yes

For local testing, open config.js and change two lines:

proxy: './api/ocr.php',   →   proxy: null,
gemini: ''                →   gemini: 'AIza...your key...'

Free key, no card, ~1500 requests a day: aistudio.google.com/apikey

Only do this on your own machine. In local mode the key sits in the page source where anyone can read it. For a public site use api/config.php, which keeps the key on the server — but that needs PHP hosting, so start.bat won’t run it.

What to tell me

  1. Do the red boxes land on single letters? If they cut letters in half or swallow two at once, that is the segmentation and it is fixable.
  2. What percentage of words matched? Under about 30% means the letter splitting is struggling with your hand.
  3. Does the output look like your writing? Print it, put it beside something you actually wrote, and look.
  4. What is the first thing that looks obviously fake? Spacing, letters too uniform, sitting too high or low? Each has a different fix.

Send the style .json if something looks wrong — it contains the extracted letters and shows exactly what was captured.

Everything runs on your machine. No page, photo or style is uploaded anywhere — unless you turn on automatic reading above, which sends the photo to Google and nothing else.
